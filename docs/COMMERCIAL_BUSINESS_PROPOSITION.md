## 1. Executive Summary: The Market Opportunity in Bangladesh Banking

Bangladesh's banking sector comprises **61 Scheduled Commercial Banks** managing over **৳18,00,000+ Crore ($150 Billion USD)** in total customer deposits.

### The Pain Point in the Industry
1. **Extreme Rate Fragmentation**: Every bank independently issues PDF interest rate circulars, changes product schedules monthly, and modifies terms across hundreds of branches.
2. **Regulatory Shift**: Under tight monetary policy (Policy Repo Rate at 10.00%), deposit rate spread between AAA-rated prime banks (e.g. Pubali @ 8.25%) and liquidity-stressed banks (e.g. National Bank / SBAC @ 11.50%–12.50%) creates significant corporate treasury arbitrage opportunities.
3. **Absence of Unified Data Infrastructure**: Prior to BDRIP-API™, there was **zero automated API infrastructure** tracking all 61 banks. Financial institutions and neobanks spent hundreds of manual operational hours calling branches or reading stale newspaper scans.
